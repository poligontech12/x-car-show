01 · ASSUMPTIONS
No existing brand or codebase was supplied, so the system is built from motorsport print rather than app convention: scrutineering sheets, timing towers, paddock signage.

Photography is the product — every image area in the prototype is a live drop slot. Drag a photo onto any placeholder and it sticks.

Twelve real builds are modelled end to end against a 142-car roster: JDM, German, muscle, stance, off-road, and the Dacia/ARO classics that actually turn up in Bucovina.
02 · WHAT DRIVES THE DESIGN
The car is the profile. The owner is a card at the foot of the car's page, never the other way round. One person, many cars, one garage.

Numbers are the ornament. Mono carries every spec, time, vote and stand number; nothing decorative sits on top of it.

One red, three jobs. Live/now, your vote, the primary action. Everything else is black, white and hairline.

No cards, no corners. Full-bleed photos and 1px rules instead of rounded containers — the chrome gets out of the photograph's way.
03 · OPEN QUESTIONS FOR YOU
— Who assigns stand numbers, owners or marshals? It changes the last step of onboarding.
