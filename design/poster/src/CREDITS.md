# Poster source imagery

Same rule as `demo-photos/`: provenance travels with the picture, so no
image in this repo is an anonymous JPEG somebody found.

## wheel-s14-cc0.jpg

- **Source** — <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:S14_03.jpg>
- **Author** — Jacksonpuaminghow
- **Licence** — [CC0 1.0](http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/deed.en)
- **Original** — 1440 × 1123, downloaded at full resolution

CC0 is why this one can go on a printed poster at all. It carries no
attribution line to reproduce and no share-alike clause reaching back onto
the artwork, which the CC BY and CC BY-SA files in `demo-photos/` both
would. If this photograph is ever swapped out, check the replacement's
licence before it goes anywhere near a printer.

It is a stranger's wheel, not an entrant's car. `scripts/poster.mjs` crops
it to a detail and strips the colour so it reads as texture rather than as
a claim about which cars are in the field. A photograph from a previous
edition, or from an entrant who agrees to it, would be better on every
count — this is the stand-in until there is one.
